# Scope and claims

The result concerns the entanglement of the same globally Haar-random pure state before and after deterministic gates on fixed boundary factors. It is a covariance law for shrinking entropy fluctuations. [Theorem](../theory/THEOREM.md) states the assumptions and [Proof](../theory/PROOF.md) gives the derivation.

| Claim | Mathematical or empirical status | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| The relative gate's operator Schmidt probabilities determine every fixed positive-order entropy covariance | Asymptotic theorem | Balanced complex-Haar input; fixed active support, orders and finite gate family |
| Centered entropies multiplied by half-dimension have a joint Gaussian limit | Finite-family theorem | Fixed finite collection of gates and positive entropy orders |
| Same-state mixed-order covariance and marginal variance have closed forms | Telescoping consequence | Fixed positive orders; limiting coefficients for the same Haar state |
| Analytical tail formulas bound omitted covariance and correlation series | Exact series identities and inequalities | Exact normalized spectra and coefficients; numerical evaluation is not an outward-rounded certificate |
| Fixed spatial access leaves positive limiting same-order correlation | Theorem consequence | The absolute entropy fluctuations still shrink |
| Dual-unitary gates attain the correlation bound | Theorem consequence | Equal active dimensions |
| Equal operator purity can coexist with unequal memory at other orders | Exact analytical examples | Operator purity is only the second moment |
| An ideal finite integer-order hierarchy identifies finite-rank operator probabilities | Exact triangular inversion | Recovery of probabilities; poor conditioning, especially near degenerate spectra |
| Finite-dimensional Haar purity correlation determines whole-half product-input entangling power | Exact identity | Purity and Rényi-2 entropy differ at finite dimension; active-factor product inputs are a different ensemble |
| Finite Haar samples illustrate the entropy law | Numerical evidence | Sample errors exclude finite-size bias; adverse diagonal discrepancies remain unresolved |
| Floquet eigenstate fluctuations disagree with the Haar prediction | Finite-cohort comparison | All twelve nontrivial comparisons fail the descriptive Haar bands in one nested circuit realization at two sizes |

The evidence and numerical interpretation are in [Results](RESULTS.md). [Gate controls](../theory/CONTROLS.md) explain the distinction between fixed boundary SWAP and whole-half SWAP.

Established annular-Wishart methods, LUE approximation, operator entanglement, entangling power and moment reconstruction are credited in [References](REFERENCES.md). The gate-spectrum reduction and actual-entropy covariance hierarchy are the result developed here.
