# Scope and current claims

Checkpoint 07, 12 September 2026.

The object is the covariance of entropy values before and after a prescribed
boundary gate on the same global Haar input. The state remains Haar marginally;
its entropy mean is unchanged, and its unscaled entropy fluctuations vanish as
1/d. This is not a nonequilibrium entanglement-growth theory.

## Assumptions

- Complex Haar input on C^d tensor C^d; a balanced bipartition.
- Deterministic unitary on fixed active dimensions r by s, with identity spectators.
- d tends to infinity through multiples of r and s; active dimensions stay fixed.
- A fixed finite set of gates and fixed positive Rényi orders; natural logarithms.
- For multiple gates use U_l U_m^dagger. Separate spectra of U_l and U_m are insufficient.

## Project boundaries and research style

Entanglement Temporal Fluctuations owns this theorem and its evidence.
Entanglement-Trajectories and Boundary-Entangling-Susceptibility remain independent.
Do not modify or import scientific claims from those projects as unstated premises.
The weak-link, bath, angle-law and multiobjective-control pilots are not active tasks.

Favor a compact physical insight with modest theory and modest numerics. Freeze
sampling choices before generating data. Preserve adverse results, source hashes,
limit order, and exact versus asymptotic distinctions. Use computation to resolve
concrete uncertainties rather than to chase favorable agreement.
