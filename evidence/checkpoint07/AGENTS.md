# Research workspace rules

Read README.md, PROJECT_SCOPE.md, PROJECT_STATUS.md, and NEXT.md before new work.
Keep Entanglement-Trajectories and Boundary-Entangling-Susceptibility separate.
Make normal reversible research and engineering decisions within the user's
current authorization; do not add unnecessary permission checkpoints.

Distinguish a theorem, internal proof review, finite-degree diagnostic, finite-size
sampling result, and conjecture. Cite primary sources. Preserve earlier evidence
and frozen protocols; a change of claim belongs in the current status and a new
checkpoint, not in retroactive changes to prediction records.

Avoid broad simulation campaigns unless a concrete scientific question needs one.
Prefer compact analytical discriminators and independent formulations. When
producing a new checkpoint, record scope, stopping rule, source hashes, results,
limitations, and enough relative-path commands to reproduce the work.
